Decisions

  • Events are not colour-coded by kind (no rainbow): the kind is an icon in the peek, Sundays are a soft filled chip.
  • My duties carry a pink rule; „something missing“ is an amber dot, a problem a red dot – always repeated in words in the peek.
  • Clicking an event opens an anchored peek card that flips away from the edge (the Sunday column is on the right, a side drawer would hide it). Enter opens the full page.
  • Today is the one pink circle in the grid.
  • Phone defaults to Seznam (agenda); Měsíc is one tap away.
